Welcome to The Tusker

A small resort built by hand, around one tree.

The Tusker is a single timber tree house standing on hand-cut poles, with a woven palm roof and a wide open deck that looks straight into the canopy. Everything you see was shaped on site by local craftsmen.

Downstairs, our 20-seat restaurant serves village Sri Lankan cooking plated with real care — and if you would like, you cook it with us.

The Restaurant

Village recipes, plated like fine dining.

Twenty seats, one kitchen, and cooking that comes straight out of the village: butterfly-pea rice, jackfruit, coconut sambol, curry leaf and clay-pot spice. Our chef plates every dish by hand.

Please share meal requests before arrival — everything is cooked to order for the guests we have that day.

Experiences

Five things we arrange ourselves.

01

Boat Tour

A slow ride out on the water — best at first light, when the birds are still feeding along the bank.

02

Handmade Batik

Sit with a batik artist, draw with hot wax and dye your own cloth to take home.

03

Village Tour

Walk the paddy paths with a local guide: home gardens, the tank bund and everyday village life.

04

Live Cooking & Cookery Class

Grind the spices, temper the coconut oil, cook village food on a clay hearth — then sit down and eat it.

05

Bicycle Tour

Bicycles for the back roads — flat, shaded lanes past tanks, fields and small temples.
