# SEO Launch Checklist

Use this after the website is uploaded to the final live domain.

1. Replace `https://your-domain.com/` in `sitemap.xml.template` with the final domain, then save it as `sitemap.xml`.
2. Add the final sitemap URL to `robots.txt`.
3. Add a canonical URL to `index.html` once the final domain is known:
   `<link rel="canonical" href="https://your-domain.com/">`
4. Open Google Search Console, verify the domain, submit `sitemap.xml`, and request indexing for the home page.
5. Create or update the Google Business Profile for The Tusker Resort and Restaurant with the same phone, email, address, photos and website URL.
6. Use HTTPS on the final website. Do not launch on plain HTTP.
7. Replace any third-party travel photos with licensed or owner-shot photos before final public launch.
8. Ask real guests to review the business on Google and travel platforms. Do not add fake reviews.
9. Keep the page updated with accurate packages, check-in details, contact details and fresh photos.
10. Test the final live URL with PageSpeed Insights and Google Rich Results Test.
